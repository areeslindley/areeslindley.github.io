About Me

Data Scientist and Statistical Methodologist transforming complex data into meaningful insights

🎯 Professional Summary

I currently work as a Senior Statistical Methodologist at the Office for National Statistics, where I specialize in time series analysis and seasonal adjustment of national statistics. My work involves developing and implementing advanced statistical methods to ensure the accuracy and reliability of critical economic indicators.

Previously, I worked as a Senior Data Scientist at the Government Office for Science, providing global insights on science and technology trends. I hold a PhD in Physics from the University of Warwick, specializing in Inertial Confinement Fusion and Computational Plasma Physics.

My expertise spans statistical modeling, machine learning, and computational physics, with a particular focus on time series analysis, seasonal adjustment techniques, and the development of innovative data science solutions for government and research applications.
