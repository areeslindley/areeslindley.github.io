Six Nations, for Significance magazine

Sports analytics · Significance

Rugby union is unusually well suited to public statistics: a small number of teams, a round-robin that everyone watches, and a scoring system people argue about in pubs. I wrote a piece for Significance on the Six Nations Championship — the RSS’s magazine for bringing statistical ideas to a wider audience.

The article itself is the thing to read; I am not reproducing it here. Related open-source rugby work, including prediction and historical analysis, lives on GitHub.
